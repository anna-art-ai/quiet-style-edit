# Nordic Style Studio

Design and build a premium personal stylist website inspired by Nordic and Danish minimalist design, with a strong editorial fashion aesthetic.

The website should feel sophisticated, calm, timeless, feminine, luxurious and intentionally designed — similar to a high-end Scandinavian fashion magazine or boutique styling studio.

VISUAL DIRECTION

- Nordic / Danish minimalism

- Quiet luxury

- Editorial fashion aesthetic

- Clean and sophisticated

- Lots of intentional whitespace

- Strong typography hierarchy

- Minimal but highly polished

- Avoid anything that feels generic, overly trendy, colorful or template-like

- Every spacing, line, border and typography detail should feel carefully considered

COLOR PALETTE

Use a warm, muted palette inspired by the provided references:

- Smoky Black: #11120D

- Olive Drab / Olive Oil: #565449

- Bone: #D8CFBC

- Floral White: #FFFBF4

- White: #FFFFFF

- Taupe: #A3968D

- Pearl: #FAF8F5

- Cocoa: #4D403A

- Khaki: #DFDACF

- Leather / Deep Charcoal: #262626

The main background should be Floral White / Pearl rather than pure white.

Use Smoky Black and Deep Charcoal for typography.

Use Olive Drab / Olive Oil as a subtle accent color.

Use Taupe, Bone and Khaki for secondary sections and visual balance.

Do NOT use bright colors.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use an elegant high-contrast editorial serif for large headings, inspired by luxury fashion magazines.

Suggested typography pairing:

- Headings: Cormorant Garamond, DM Serif Display, Playfair Display, or a similar sophisticated editorial serif

- Body/navigation: Inter, Manrope, Helvetica Neue, or another clean modern sans-serif

Large headlines should feel elegant and editorial, with generous letter spacing and thin typography.

DESIGN DETAILS

Use minimalist lines throughout the interface:

- 1px horizontal divider lines

- Thin vertical lines

- Subtle borders

- Fine geometric details

- Minimal icons

- Small uppercase labels

- Generous margins

- Asymmetrical editorial layouts where appropriate

The lines should be subtle and elegant, not decorative or excessive.

Avoid rounded cards everywhere.

Avoid excessive shadows.

Avoid gradients.

Avoid glassmorphism.

Avoid excessive animations.

Avoid large colorful buttons.

The website should feel like a carefully art-directed fashion portfolio.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. HERO SECTION

Create a full-width editorial hero.

Navigation:

- Logo / personal stylist name on the left

- ABOUT

- SERVICES

- STYLE JOURNAL

- CONTACT

- A small CTA such as "BOOK A CONSULTATION" on the right

Keep the navigation extremely minimal with lots of whitespace.

Hero:

Use a large, sophisticated fashion image of a stylish woman with an editorial / Scandinavian aesthetic.

Place elegant typography over or beside the image.

Example:

PERSONAL STYLIST

[NAME]

"Style is not about having more.

It is about knowing what belongs."

Small supporting text:

Personal styling, wardrobe editing and intentional dressing for the modern woman.

CTA:

EXPLORE SERVICES

Secondary text link:

BOOK A CONSULTATION →

Use a thin vertical line as a subtle visual element connecting the typography.

2. INTRODUCTION / PHILOSOPHY

Create a very spacious section with a small uppercase label:

THE PHILOSOPHY

Large editorial statement:

"Your wardrobe should feel

as considered as the life

you are building."

Below it, add a short paragraph explaining the stylist's philosophy:

personal style, simplicity, confidence, quality over quantity, timeless pieces and intentional dressing.

Use an asymmetric layout with a thin vertical divider.

3. SERVICES

Create a refined services section.

Title:

SERVICES

Show 3 or 4 services in a minimalist editorial layout rather than traditional cards.

Example:

01

WARDROBE EDIT

A considered edit of your existing wardrobe, helping you remove what no longer serves you and discover what truly works.

02

PERSONAL STYLING

A personalized styling experience built around your lifestyle, proportions, personality and aesthetic.

03

SHOPPING & CURATION

Thoughtfully selected pieces that complement your wardrobe and create a cohesive personal style.

04

OCCASION STYLING

Refined looks for events, travel, celebrations and important moments.

Each service should be separated by thin horizontal lines.

On hover, subtly reveal an image or change the typography/olive accent.

4. EDITORIAL IMAGE SECTION

Create a large editorial photography section inspired by luxury fashion campaigns.

Use neutral, warm photography:

- tailored clothing

- linen

- leather accessories

- minimalist interiors

- neutral outfits

- olive, beige, cream and black tones

- natural light

Overlay a small editorial caption:

THE ART OF LESS

"Less noise. More intention."

5. ABOUT THE STYLIST

Create an asymmetric two-column section.

One side:

Large portrait/editorial photograph.

Other side:

ABOUT THE STYLIST

[NAME]

A refined personal introduction describing the stylist's approach and experience.

Include a minimal text link:

READ MY STORY →

Use a vertical divider between the image and text.

6. STYLE JOURNAL

Create an editorial magazine-style section.

Title:

STYLE JOURNAL

Display 3 featured articles with large photography and minimal text.

Examples:

THE CAPSULE WARDROBE

How to build a wardrobe that works harder with less.

THE ART OF NEUTRALS

Why a muted palette creates effortless style.

DRESSING WITH INTENTION

A modern approach to personal style.

Use numbers such as:

01 / 02 / 03

Keep everything minimal and editorial.

7. TESTIMONIAL SECTION

Use a very clean section with a large quote.

"Working with [NAME] completely changed the way I see my wardrobe."

— CLIENT NAME

Use a large serif quotation and lots of whitespace.

8. CTA SECTION

Create a darker full-width section using Smoky Black #11120D.

Typography should be Floral White.

Headline:

YOUR STYLE,

CONSIDERED.

Supporting text:

Let's create a wardrobe that feels unmistakably you.

CTA:

BOOK A PRIVATE CONSULTATION →

Use Olive Drab #565449 subtly for the button or small accent.

9. FOOTER

Minimal Scandinavian footer.

Include:

- Logo / stylist name

- Instagram

- Pinterest

- Email

- CONTACT

- SERVICES

- ABOUT

- STYLE JOURNAL

- PRIVACY

Use thin divider lines.

Bottom:

© 2026 [NAME] — ALL RIGHTS RESERVED

INTERACTION & ANIMATION

Animations should be subtle and premium:

- Smooth fade-in on scroll

- Gentle image reveal

- Slight image scale on hover

- Elegant underline animations

- Smooth navigation transitions

- Subtle page transitions

Do not use flashy animations.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop:

- Large editorial layouts

- Generous whitespace

- Asymmetrical image/text compositions

Tablet:

- Preserve the editorial feeling while simplifying layouts.

Mobile:

- Elegant stacked sections

- Large typography

- Full-width imagery

- Simple hamburger navigation

- Maintain generous spacing

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The final result should NOT look like a typical personal stylist template.

It should feel like a combination of:

- Scandinavian luxury

- Danish interior design

- High-fashion editorial

- Modern personal branding

- Quiet luxury

- Minimal architecture

Think: "less, but better."

The design should communicate:

CONFIDENCE + ELEGANCE + SIMPLICITY + PERSONALITY.

Use the provided visual references as inspiration for the color palette, typography, spacing, editorial composition, minimalist lines and overall atmosphere, but create an original design rather than copying any reference exactly.
